Behind the scenes

How our AI agents work

01
🧠

Supervisor & Guardrail

An intelligent supervisor validates your request, selects the right agents, and extracts trip constraints automatically.

02
✈️

Flight Agent

Connects to AviationStack via MCP to retrieve real-time airport, airline, and route information.

03
🏨

Hotel Agent

Searches for accommodations using Tavily's web-search MCP to find the best options for your budget.

04
☁️

Weather Agent

Fetches live weather and 5-day forecasts from OpenWeatherMap to help you pack smart.

05
📋

Itinerary & Final

Synthesises all information into a detailed, day-by-day travel plan polished for your approval.

06
✅

Human-in-the-Loop

You review the draft and provide feedback. The AI then finalises the plan based on your decisions.
